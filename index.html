Beware of Fraudulent Schemes

Misusing the Name of IEL

Fraudulent individuals and entities are increasingly misusing the identity of licensed Securities Brokers through fake profiles, apps, unauthorized WhatsApp numbers, and misleading social media pages to deceive investors.

WHAT TO WATCH OUT FOR

Fake WhatsApp Numbers & Apps

Unauthorized mobile apps and WhatsApp numbers impersonating licensed brokers.

Fake Social Media Profiles

Profiles falsely impersonating directors or executives of brokerage houses.

Requests for OTPs & Funds

Demands for OTPs, personal information, or money through unofficial channels.

Misleading Pages & Links

Fake pages with no official affiliation to any PSX-licensed brokerage firm.

PLEASE NOTE

  • IEL has NO affiliation with any unofficial pages, profiles, apps, or WhatsApp numbers.
  • We NEVER request OTPs, personal information, funds, or investments through unofficial platforms.
  • All communication is conducted ONLY through our official phone numbers, website, email, and verified social media handles.

HOW TO STAY PROTECTED

  • Always verify the authenticity of any communication before engaging.
  • Confirm licensed entities through the SECP and PSX official websites before transacting.
  • Transact ONLY through official banking channels linked to licensed brokers.
  • Regularly visit SECP, PSX, CDC, and NCCPL websites for authentic updates and public alerts.
  • When in doubt, contact our official representatives directly before taking any action.
